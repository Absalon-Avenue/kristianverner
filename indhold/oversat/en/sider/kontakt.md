Write a few lines about what you have in mind. It might be a particular work you have seen here, or an idea for something that does not exist yet.

I answer myself. Often it is easiest to talk on the phone or meet in the studio, so I can hear about the space, the light and what the work needs to do.

Every work is made from scratch and by hand. That is why it always starts with a conversation.
