Hyperbaton is a figure of speech that marks an inversion of the normal order of words. The consequence of a hyperbaton is a new weighting of the words that goes beyond the expected.

In a play between form and content, the mobile is a material reflection on being dyslexic. Minthe is himself dyslexic. Like a giant sentence diagram, the mobile’s heavy levels move inconsistently, imperfectly and creatively.
